00 / What matters

Can it finish the work?

An official integration is not automatically useful. The grade starts with the route an operator can connect today, what it can change, and whether it completes the recurring job without returning to the manual product.

Human job, by category
Media needs Read, Create, Modify and Delete. Analytics, measurement, creative and infrastructure need different tests.
Connection effort
Cloud projects, developer tokens, OAuth applications, custom code and allowlists lower daily usefulness.
Market role
Reference, Emerging and Specialist describe the player's place in the field. They do not determine its grade.
Evidence basis
Documentation reviewed unless an entry states otherwise. A Production readiness label is not proof of hands-on execution.
