# Refresh both indexes around real execution

**Replace the current rankings and copy before sharing the existing graphic.** The review covers all **60 entries**, with fresh documentation-based assessments for **58** and explicit pending status for **Meta Ads and The Trade Desk**. Several tools now perform meaningful writes that the existing index still describes as read-only, while some highly graded APIs still require custom engineering before an external assistant can use them. The revised measure should combine useful reading, actual execution and connection effort, rather than awarding category-specific A grades that imply a comparable level of control. The synthesis proposes **33 grade changes**, including changes caused by the new rubric rather than a product regression. No account was connected, no live write was executed, and none of these grades certifies reliability or complete interface parity.

## Show the route and its three dimensions before the grade

The question is how much recurring work an external assistant can complete without returning to the platform. **Read, write and setup therefore belong in every comparison row**, with create, edit, delete and publish separated inside the detailed assessment. The object matters: changing a campaign budget, creating a research task, saving a model and submitting answer feedback are different kinds of writes. The reviewed Fivetran route illustrates this distinction: it queries a prepared context layer with read-only SQL and accepts feedback, but that feedback is not permission to change the warehouse or pipelines. ([Fivetran tools](https://fivetran.com/docs/context-layer/agent-context-mcp))

A native directory app is the easiest connection only for the clients and regions actually supported. Mixpanel documents direct Claude/ChatGPT links for the US, while EU and India require regional custom connectors. Adobe CJA has a Claude directory connector; Adobe Analytics uses a manually configured hosted URL. A hosted endpoint also does not erase administrator preparation: Microsoft Advertising still requires an Entra application for the ChatGPT/Claude route, while Snowflake needs an administrator to expose the intended tools and underlying data. ([Mixpanel connection guide](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/mcp), [Adobe Claude guide](https://developer.adobe.com/analytics-mcp/docs/guides/claude), [Microsoft setup](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/mcp-setup?view=bingads-13), [Snowflake managed MCP](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-agents-mcp))

Under the proposed combined rubric, **A-range grades require useful reads, substantial execution and practical connection**. B-range grades describe bounded execution, material setup or important workflow gaps. Read-only research and custom-API-only routes sit at C/C+ even when their underlying product is excellent. This is a changed editorial measure, not a benchmark of output quality, accuracy or business results. Ahrefs, Semrush and Similarweb should therefore be presented as strong research connections with a clearly bounded operational score. Semrush explicitly excludes project creation and settings changes from its MCP. ([Ahrefs tool categories](https://docs.ahrefs.com/en/mcp/docs/tool-categories), [Semrush MCP](https://developer.semrush.com/api/v3/introduction/semrush-mcp/), [Similarweb connector](https://docs.similarweb.com/api-v5/mcp-integration/available-integrations/claude-integration))

The record proposals preserve a separate readiness label and do not convert unknown capabilities into a zero score. **Meta Ads and The Trade Desk retain their previous grade and verified date, visibly marked pending and excluded from confident ranking.** Meta's vendor pages were inaccessible in this research; The Trade Desk's announcement establishes OAK's private-beta direction but not the current tool inventory. An inaccessible source is neither proof that a capability disappeared nor a reason to assert new capabilities from another API. ([OAK release notes](https://partner.thetradedesk.com/v3/portal/api/doc/ReleaseNotes))

## Advertising now has bounded writes and clearer setup trade-offs

**Google Ads is no longer accurately described as read-only.** Its official MCP supports standalone assets, local video uploads and labels, but still cannot perform ordinary campaign, budget, bid or ad edits. Its remote option is an operator deployment rather than a Google-hosted connector. The developer-token prerequisite also needs removal following Google's documented September transition to Cloud-project access. B− remains appropriate for useful diagnosis and narrow changes with substantial setup. ([Google MCP guide](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/developer-toolkit/mcp-server), [official source](https://github.com/googleads/google-ads-mcp), [access transition](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/api-policy/developer-token))

TikTok documents campaign creation and changes to budgets, bids and targeting, but exact deletion and separate activation tools were not established by the reviewed help page. Its A becomes **A− with those gaps visible**. X's hosted MCP documents separate campaign and line-item activation, with new objects initially paused, and selected deletion operations; its developer-app setup keeps it at B+. Amazon's multi-step campaign actions are useful but remain open beta with partner/API onboarding. Snapchat has moved to self-service access, yet its MCP remains explicitly read-only and still needs organisation approval plus individual consent. ([TikTok MCP](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-tiktok-for-business-mcp-server?lang=en), [X MCP tools](https://docs.x.com/x-ads-api/mcp), [Amazon announcement](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/news/amazon-ads-mcp-server-open-beta), [Snapchat scope and access](https://developers.snap.com/marketing-api/Ads-MCP/Introduction))

DV360's **12 November update** adds responsive YouTube ad creation, updates and deletion in v5 beta. That corrects an overly broad old limitation, but does not supply a ready external-agent workflow or cover every YouTube format. CM360, DV360, Apple, LinkedIn, Pinterest and Reddit remain custom API routes in this assessment. Their C/C+ grades express connection burden and verified action boundaries, not reduced API quality. Search Ads 360's evaluated Reporting API is itself read-only. ([DV360 announcement](https://developers.google.com/ads/blog/posts/2026/11/manage-responsive-youtube-ads-with-display), [DV360 v5 reference](https://developers.google.com/display-video/api/reference/rest/v5/advertisers.lineItems), [SA360 Reporting API](https://developers.google.com/search-ads/reporting))

An intermediary can now be easier for a marketer than a platform's own integration. **Supermetrics adds opt-in campaign writes** across supported platforms, including creation in a paused state and supported budget, status, setting and ad changes. Its A− reflects native chat setup plus execution, with account entitlement and per-platform gaps still explicit. New campaigns starting paused is a useful boundary; it must not become the unsupported claim that every later action always requires human confirmation, because approval policy is configurable. ([Write access](https://docs.supermetrics.com/docs/how-to-enable-campaign-write-access), [campaign operations](https://docs.supermetrics.com/v1/docs/how-to-manage-ad-campaigns-with-ai-tools))

## CMS scores should follow the complete editorial operation

**WordPress.com belongs in the top group**, with native ChatGPT/Claude connections and documented post/page drafting, editing, publication and trash operations. Its A is for that routine lifecycle, not every possible site operation. The page must also show that editing published content can affect the live site immediately and that temporary free-site access ends on **30 November 2026**. Webflow's A− is supported by explicit CMS draft, edit, publish, unpublish and delete tools; most routine CMS work does not require the Designer open, although several visual tasks still do. ([WordPress.com guide](https://developer.wordpress.com/docs/mcp/), [WordPress.com tools](https://developer.wordpress.com/docs/mcp/tools-reference/), [access requirements](https://wordpress.com/support/mcp/), [Webflow data tools](https://developers.webflow.com/mcp/tools/data-tools), [Webflow limitations](https://developers.webflow.com/mcp/reference/overview))

Contentful remains A− for broad content and release operations through hosted OAuth, with configuration required for each environment. Sanity also remains A−, but the description needs a significant correction: individual draft publication is supported, while release scheduling, publication, archival and deletion remain outside the documented release tools. Draft/release-version deletion cannot be presented as general published-document deletion, and `liveEdit` types have different draft semantics. Directus supports broad item operations and OAuth, but the project schema determines what draft and publish mean; self-hosted OAuth adds configuration and restart work. ([Contentful reference](https://www.contentful.com/developers/docs/mcp/model-context-protocol-mcp-server/), [Sanity authoring MCP](https://www.sanity.io/docs/ai/mcp-server), [Directus tools](https://directus.com/docs/guides/ai/mcp/tools), [Directus OAuth](https://directus.com/docs/guides/ai/mcp/oauth))

Shopify's official AI Toolkit now connects to authenticated store execution through the local CLI. It earns B for a real route to Admin GraphQL reads and opt-in mutations, while retaining the runtime, authentication and scope burden. Its developer-documentation MCP is a separate interface. WooCommerce also earns B for useful product operations, but its 11.3 migration removes the old endpoint and REST-key authentication; the WordPress MCP Adapter must now be installed separately. Product creation and deletion are documented, while safe draft defaults and publication semantics still need verification against the installed ability schema. ([Shopify Toolkit](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/ai-toolkit), [Shopify store execution](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/shopify-cli/store/store-execute), [WooCommerce guide](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/features/mcp/), [11.3 migration](https://developer.woocommerce.com/2026/10/21/woocommerce-11-3-mcp/))

BigCommerce's B− explicitly evaluates **early-access merchant product-content tools**, not the generally available shopper MCP. It creates hidden products and edits supported product content, but the documented inventory does not provide page/blog CMS tools or product publication/deletion. WordPress MCP Adapter receives C because exposing installed abilities is infrastructure, not proof of a complete editorial assistant. Adobe Commerce also receives C for a capable but custom traditional Commerce/Magento REST integration; that scope must not be generalised to the separate Cloud Service product. ([BigCommerce Store Management](https://docs.bigcommerce.com/developer/api-reference/mcp/store-management), [WordPress adapter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WordPress/mcp-adapter/trunk/README.md), [Adobe REST scope](https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/rest/))

## Conclusion

The strongest public index will make its limits useful. A marketer should be able to see whether a route can carry out the next action, which objects it can change, and what setup or manual handoff remains. That clarity is more valuable than preserving a visually tidy ranking based on incompatible category scores. The update should therefore ship with a visible rubric-change date, pending badges for the two blocked reviews, and refreshed sharing artwork that does not imply an exact order among tied grades.

The proposed records and French copy are editorial implementation inputs, not a released snapshot. Auxiliary CLI/plugin metadata preserved from the previous catalogue was not independently re-certified unless the route research explicitly refreshed it; the new verification date applies to the named evaluated route. Production readiness remains a documentation judgement. Repository validation, deployment, authenticated tool discovery and a controlled successful action are distinct proof stages, and none should be implied by the evidence label on this page.
